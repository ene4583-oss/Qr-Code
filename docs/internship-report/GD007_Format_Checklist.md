Source: supplied GD-FES-ITC-007, revision 6, effective 1 April 2024.

| Requirement | Guideline | Current interim report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page/font | A4; Times New Roman 12 | Applied to report body; original official forms retained |
| Paragraphs | Justified; subsection first paragraph flush left; subsequent paragraphs indent 1.27 cm | Applied to body |
| Spacing | 1.5-line title-to-text and paragraph spacing | Body line spacing 1.5, 18-pt paragraph/title gap; final visual check needed |
| Chapters | Numbered sections; each chapter starts a new page | Retained and checked |

| Appendices | Relevant supporting details | Appendix A retained; B/C not required and not reintroduced |
| Log records | Weekly/monthly activities, systematic records | Current two weeks; later weeks/month summary blank |
| Assessment/rubrics | Compulsory attachment | Original official assessment material retained; assessor fields blank |
| Length | Around 6,000–8,000 words and minimum 30 to around 40 pages for body texts (Chapters 1, 2 and 3) | English Chapters 1–3 currently approx. 1,668 words; interim, not full placement |

The guide does not explicitly say the entire document, including cover, appendices, logs and rubrics, has a hard 40-page maximum. Its stated target is the main body. Confirm any newer faculty-specific rule with the supervisor. Word counts use a local text estimate, not Word's exact counter. Full page counts have not been rendered or certified.

Final completion still needs later actual training work, company confirmation, verified evidence, the student's review/signature and assessor input. Do not fill missing word/page targets with fabricated activities. If a separate instruction from the faculty requires a total-document cap, apply that additional rule to the final layout.
